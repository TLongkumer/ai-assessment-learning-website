Step 1

Students Use Google AI Studio With a System Prompt

The assignment begins before the student writes anything. The teacher first sets the behavior of the AI: it must tutor, question, scaffold, and delay final answer production. Students then follow a separate instruction sheet so their interaction becomes assessable learning evidence.

Setup Checklist

  • Open Google AI Studio.
  • Paste the system prompt into system instructions.
  • Use the latest Gemini model available to the student.
  • Keep reasoning low and turn off extra tools/options unless instructed.
  • Begin only after setup by saying: "Let's start".

Student instruction file

The assignment tells students that process is the assessment.

The instructions for the Ethics of Care assignment make the rule explicit: students are not mainly assessed for getting the right answer or producing the most polished write-up. They are assessed for how purposefully they use AI to learn.

Students are expected to show that they can:

  • Use AI purposefully rather than passively.
  • Guide the conversation with meaningful questions.
  • Learn from AI responses and apply that learning later.
  • Think critically about what the AI says.
  • Apply concepts to real examples and case studies.

System prompt architecture

The prompt turns AI into a structured tutor.

The sample system prompt is for deontological ethics, but the pattern can be adapted to other topics. Its most important rule is that AI should not immediately provide a finished essay, case study, or full argument. It must first create a meaningful learning dialogue.

Persona

Educational AI Tutor

Specialist role, subject focus, and mission to develop reasoning rather than repeat definitions.

Golden rule

No instant final answer

Requires a minimum meaningful exchange before helping produce a final synthesis or draft.

Teaching process

Clarify, scaffold, test

Activates prior knowledge, introduces concepts, asks for examples, and invites counterarguments.

Final stage

Synthesize student thinking

Only later does AI organize the student's ideas into an outline or essay draft.

How students should interact

Good interaction means the student leads.

Do

  • Ask follow-up questions.
  • Request clarification when ideas are unclear.
  • Ask AI to compare concepts.
  • Question oversimplified answers.
  • Use examples to test understanding.
  • Explain later learning in your own words.

Do Not

  • Ask AI to write the whole answer immediately.
  • Give one prompt and copy the response.
  • Only answer AI questions without asking your own.
  • Accept AI praise as proof of strong work.
  • Submit polished writing without the learning process.

After the conversation

The transcript stays part of the assignment.

Once students believe they have achieved the objectives, they ask AI to create a final essay from the conversation. They then revise structure, argument, language, and flow. The final essay matters, but the transcript is what shows whether AI was used as a learning partner.
